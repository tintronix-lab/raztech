# RazTech Innovations AB

**Innovating tomorrow, today**

RazTech Innovations is a Swedish IoT product studio and consultancy, working with companies across Europe. We build connected products and apps of our own, and we help industry, cities and property owners turn sensors, real-time data and AI into services people trust.

This repository holds our website.

- **English:** <https://raztech.duckdns.org/>
- **Svenska:** <https://raztech.duckdns.org/sv/>

## What we do

- **Our own products.** Eight of them, in four areas: city and mobility, home and nature, care and work, and play.
- **IoT and industrial IoT.** From sensor to decision: devices and firmware, connectivity, edge and cloud, analytics, and security.
- **Consulting.** Product strategy, connected products, smart cities, AI, digital transformation, accessible and regulated software, and sustainable product design.

The website has the details.
